# Wisdom registry

Status: registry specification. No research wisdom is preaccepted in this fresh campaign.

Wisdom is an actionable principle derived from a synthesis of tested knowledge. It guides the next diagnosis or experiment. A successful run, a persuasive explanation, or a high proxy score is insufficient.

Each accepted item must record its applicable conditions, linked accepted knowledge, executable checks, recommended decision, uncertainty, and known counterexamples. It must name source revisions and evidence identities. Keep the prose here and its machine-readable registry link in the record system.

Before retrieval, verify status and scope. If no wisdom applies, fall back to tested knowledge. If that is also absent, inspect the evidence and state that the next hypothesis is new. Do not turn an unmatched case into an apparent match by dropping its design conditions.

Revise or suspend an item when contradictory evidence changes its support. Keep previous versions. A failed intervention remains in the evidence archive even when a later principle supersedes the earlier interpretation.

The supplied `SOUL.md`, Core belief 8, defines these standards. This directory's existence does not certify any scientific claim.
